Two charge profiles

Choose the mode that matches your device.

Press the button once to switch between white and blue light modes before charging. Each mode uses a different full-charge recognition profile.

White light mode

Best for phones, tablets, laptops, and other devices with displays or active standby behaviour. After the device reaches 100%, ChargeGuard allows the final top-off phase, then cuts power automatically.

Blue light mode

Best for smaller non-screen devices such as earbuds, shavers, power banks, mini fans, beauty tools, and portable accessories. It is tuned for faster post-full disconnection.
